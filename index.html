Internship schedule

Pick a day to open that week and edit it.

Drag down a day to add hours. Drag a block to move it, pull an edge to resize, or tap it to change the colour. The mark on a day header copies that whole day.

Sync with Google

Your schedule is saved to a private folder in your own Google Drive, so it follows you between your laptop and your phone. Nobody else can see it, and it never touches anyone else's server.
